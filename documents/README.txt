documents/

- `00_General_Information_for_Participants.pdf`: sent to participants by email upon registration; describes the study procedure, duration, and compensation prior to the recording session.
- `01_Declaration_of_Consent.pdf`: informed consent form, signed by participants prior to participation.
- `02_Codeword_Instructions.pdf`: instructions for generating a personal anonymization codeword, used to link participant data without storing identifying information.
- `03_Baseline_Questionnaire.pdf`: administered before the recording session; collects data such as handedness, sleep, caffeine intake, neurofeedback experience…
- `04_Experience_Questionnaire.pdf`: administered just after the recording session; collects participants' subjective experience during the session.
- `CRED-nf_Checklist.pdf`: completed CRED-nf checklist (Ros et al., 2020) for this study.
- `Experiment_Protocol.pdf`: instructions for running the experiment: starting and ending a session, recording the different data streams, and verifying that the continuous feedback sound is working correctly. Written for the Neuropsychology Lab systems, but it might be useful for other setups.
- `Participant_Instructions.pdf`: instructions presented to participants during the experimental task (Supplementary Material B of the paper). Compiled as plain text from `ptb_demo.m` (`code/experiment`) for easier reading.
